What Is an AP World Score Calculator?

An AP World Score Calculator is a tool that estimates your final AP score (1–5) based on your performance in the following sections:

  • Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
  • Short Answer Questions (SAQs)
  • Document-Based Question (DBQ)
  • Long Essay Question (LEQ)

The calculator uses the latest exam weighting system to provide an estimated score.

Why Use an AP World Score Calculator?

  • Instant score estimation
  • Better exam preparation
  • Identify weak areas
  • Set realistic AP score goals

How AP World Scores Are Calculated

Multiple Choice Section

Accounts for approximately 40% of the total score.

Short Answer Questions

Accounts for approximately 20% of the total score.

Document-Based Question (DBQ)

Accounts for approximately 25% of the total score.

Long Essay Question (LEQ)

Accounts for approximately 15% of the total score.

The final composite score is converted into an AP score from 1 to 5.
